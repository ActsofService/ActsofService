title: "Avtryck: Graduation Identity"
description: >-
  The graduating show is a place to make a mark (avtryck), a chance to take
  place and position one self for the future with competence and self-assertion.
  We have made the choice to portray this year's theme with a seal as a stamp of
  quality and with a signet ring as a symbol for belonging and community: We are
  the graduating bachelor class at Beckmans College of Design 2026. The seal is
  a ceremonial and bold/daring way to make one's mark with and is the soul of
  the graduating show’s campaign. 


  The campaign was created together with Linn Sehlstedt and Phoebe Crookes.
