title: Helvete
descriptionEnglish: >-
  A visual identity project from Olle Ljung’s second year at Beckmans. The brief
  was to reimagine the visual identity of a discontinued company and reconsider
  what its purpose could be today.


  Olle chose to reimagine the infamous Norwegian black metal record store “Helvete”, which was open during the 1990s and became associated with violence and controversy. He transformed it from a record store and meeting place into an electronic music album project.


  The project explores and reflects on Helvete’s complex history while also creating a tribute to the cultural significance of the place. Olle developed a logo, artwork for vinyl, CD and cassette, as well as a design manual. Spegel (Erling Swedenmark) contributed to the project by producing the music for the album.
