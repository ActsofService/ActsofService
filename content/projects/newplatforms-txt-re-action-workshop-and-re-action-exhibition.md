  “The current state of established digital platforms and its influence on
  visual communication is under scrutiny by us students, teachers and human
  beings. Because frankly, we’re disappointed with the current state of
  affairs.” 


  Three parts created for New Platforms, an design festival at Beckmans Collage of Design. 

  NewPlatforms.txt is a publication made to broaden the subject of alternative and new possible platforms. We learn more about this subject through invited writers, who each bring an interesting point of view. Re:action Workshop is a workshop presented as an analogue internet platform. Where you can playfully engage, create and react to your surroundings. Re:action Exhibition is the result of a workshop our class completed together. We wanted to show our collective creativity and reduce the pressure of performance. To achieve less pressure and a unified expression, we wanted to use limited tools and shapes. From these thoughts came the idea of Re:action Workshop.


  Created together with Phoebe Crookes and Hanna Hedberg
