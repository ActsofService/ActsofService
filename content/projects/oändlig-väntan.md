  A stackable stool inspired by a queue ticket.


  What does it feel like to be in a particular situation, and what does that feeling look like? Through a piece of furniture, we have given physical form to the feeling of waiting in line; a frustrating system that is at the same time reassuring.


  Design collaboration: Celine Skolleborg, Ella Farestam and Olle Ljung

  Designsamarbete: Celine Skolleborg, Ella Farestam och Olle Ljung
