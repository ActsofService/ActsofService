title: Tyrvinr 
descriptionEnglish: >-
  Tyrvinr  - a mythological sword bound by both magic and a curse. The sword
  cannot be broken, cleaves through stone and iron, and is guaranteed to win in
  battle. But every time the blade leaves its sheath, a life must be claimed.
  The act also unleashes three evil deeds. Now the sword rests driven into the
  earth, a reminder of the battle, and the guilt that followed.


  Artwork displayed at PLX Tjärö 2025, a music and art festival at Tjärö, Blekinge.
